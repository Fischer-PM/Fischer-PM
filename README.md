# Stephen Fischer · Product Manager

I build enterprise messaging infrastructure and API platforms at the intersection of technical scale and regulated environments — where the hard part isn't the technology, it's making the right call under constraint and shipping on schedule.

---

## What I work on

**Platform scale in regulated environments.** I own product strategy and execution for infrastructure that operates at billions-of-messages scale, crosses regulatory boundaries, and has no tolerance for downtime. The decisions that matter are the ones where every option has a real cost.

**0→1 in constrained systems.** Two of my highest-impact launches were greenfield — a KYC/CDD guided experience that cut information collection from a week to under 20 minutes and shipped with regulatory sign-off on time, and a 0→1 agent productivity mobile app launched across a 200,000-agent global network.

**Platform credibility with engineering.** I came up through platform work. I know what it means to own an API contract, manage deprecation without breaking downstream, and protect the critical path when stakeholder pressure runs the other direction.

## What I'm thinking about

How much of the PM role is bottlenecked on the quality and speed of the thinking — and how much of that can be systematized without losing judgment. I've been building AI-assisted tooling for PMs: not to replace the decisions, but to compress the time between "I need to think through this" and "I have something worth sharing."
