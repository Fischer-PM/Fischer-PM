---

AI doesn't change what the PM job is — it changes the speed at which you can do it badly. The frameworks here are built around that premise: not "how do you use AI in your workflow" but "where does AI create real value and where does it create the illusion of progress." The distinction matters because the illusion is more dangerous than not having the tools at all — a PM who moves fast without judgment ships the wrong thing more confidently, and more often.
