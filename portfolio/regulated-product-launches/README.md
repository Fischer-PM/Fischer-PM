The hard part of regulated product work isn't the compliance — it's not using regulation as an excuse for slow execution. Every PM who has worked in a regulated environment has seen it: a timeline that exists because of a legal review that wasn't scoped correctly, a product that collected twice the data required because no one audited the original fields, a pilot that ran for six months because no one defined what success looked like before it started. Regulation defines the constraint; it doesn't define the timeline. The PM's job is to understand the constraint precisely enough to work at the edges of it, not to treat the constraint as a ceiling on what's possible.
