# Portfolio: Thinking Artifacts & Frameworks

These repositories document the frameworks, decision tools, and operational playbooks built across platform PM work at scale — in regulated environments, during rapid scaling, and across 0→1 launches. They are **thinking artifacts**, not open-source projects.

## What's here

The 6 core portfolio repos represent the recurring patterns and decision structures that separate good judgment from guesswork:

| Repo | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **[pm-platforms](https://github.com/Fischer-PM/pm-platforms)** | Decision frameworks from multi-year platform PM work at scale. Trade-off analysis on scalability vs. customization, managing technical debt, protecting the critical path. |
| **[regulated-launches](https://github.com/Fischer-PM/regulated-launches)** | Launch playbooks for 0→1 products in regulated environments. Sequencing of regulatory, technical, and go-to-market work. How to ship on schedule when approval chains run months. |
| **[ai-pm](https://github.com/Fischer-PM/ai-pm)** | Frameworks for AI product management. Thinking on evaluation, unit economics, differentiation, user value under uncertainty. |
| **[product-strategy](https://github.com/Fischer-PM/product-strategy)** | Strategic artifacts: competitive positioning, prioritization frameworks, trade-off analysis. How to reason about what not to build. |
| **[api-platforms](https://github.com/Fischer-PM/api-platforms)** | Developer portal and ecosystem strategy at scale. API contract management, deprecation strategy, scaling to billions of annual calls. |
| **[messaging-systems](https://github.com/Fischer-PM/messaging-systems)** | Large-scale messaging platform architecture and operations. Managing systems at billions-of-messages scale, reliability under constraint, operational patterns. |

## Why think this way

The work in these repos demonstrates three core principles:

1. **Systems thinking.** Products aren't features — they're constraint-solving machines. Every decision has trade-offs. The goal is to know what you're trading.

2. **Judgment under constraint.** The hard decisions don't happen when you have unlimited resources. They happen when regulatory approval, technical debt, and market timing collide. The framework is how you navigate that.

3. **Operational credibility.** Platforms live or die on API contract management, deprecation strategy, and the day-to-day reality of keeping systems running under load. The thinking has to account for that.
